# III. Company Overview

Accel Entertainment, Inc. was originally founded in 2010 and began operating in 2012 after obtaining the legal framework to distribute gambling-oriented gaming terminals. It is currently a publicly traded company listed on the New York Stock Exchange (NYSE) under the ticker symbol **ACEL**, headquartered in Burr Ridge, Illinois.

Accel is a leading provider of distributed gaming solutions, specializing in the installation, maintenance, and operation of video gaming terminals (VGTs) — including slot machines and themed casino game terminals — primarily in non-casino establishments such as bars, restaurants, convenience stores, and veteran fraternal organizations. Their business model is built on long-term, exclusive service and operational contracts with these partner locations.

## Infrastructure & Operations

Accel relies on robust IT infrastructure utilizing **Microsoft Azure** cloud services for application hosting, along with application performance management tools for monitoring all 27,180 terminals. Operations depend on proprietary gaming hardware and software platforms, with regular audits and physical maintenance performed by field service technicians.
