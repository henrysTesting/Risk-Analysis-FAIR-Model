# II. Executive Summary

This risk assessment evaluates Accel Entertainment's cybersecurity posture using the **FAIR (Factor Analysis of Information Risk)** model, focusing on three primary threat scenarios:

1. Compromise of field service technicians
2. Exploitation of the AE Accelerator application
3. Phishing of senior executives responsible for cybersecurity oversight

These scenarios were selected based on open-source intelligence (OSINT), including public internet sources and Accel Entertainment's own published filings.

Our quantitative FAIR analysis shows that, **prior to mitigation**, all three scenarios present the potential for significant financial loss, regulatory fines, and reputational damage.

After applying reasonable mitigation strategies — strict access controls for technicians, patch management and API security for AE Accelerator, and proper cybersecurity leadership — estimated losses across all scenarios were significantly reduced. Loss event frequencies and magnitudes were recalculated, and updated Loss Exceedance Curves (LECs) indicate substantial improvement in risk posture.

**Key recommendations:**
- Enhance technician cybersecurity training and access monitoring
- Institute mandatory secure development practices for AE Accelerator
- Ensure cybersecurity oversight is handled by qualified technical leadership

While Accel Entertainment faces notable cybersecurity risks, these risks are **measurable, manageable, and reducible** with proactive investment in targeted security controls and organizational accountability.
