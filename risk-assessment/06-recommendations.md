# VI. Recommendations

Accel Entertainment faces cybersecurity risks ranging from medium to severe. A multi-layered defense strategy is necessary to reduce financial exposure across all three scenarios.

---

## Scenario 1 — Service Technician Inside Attack

- **Technician Activity Logging:** Implement a centralized system that logs and monitors technician actions in real time. A machine's pre-visit state is saved as a snapshot; the post-visit state is uploaded for admin review, enabling change detection.
- **Security Awareness Training:** Mandatory training covering the dos and don'ts of operating on AE systems, including social engineering recognition.
- **Enhanced Background Checks:** Strengthen vetting procedures during the hiring process, particularly for contractor and subcontractor technicians.

---

## Scenario 2 — AE Accelerator Unpatched Vulnerability

- **Patch Management:** Establish a fixed, regular patching schedule (preferably monthly) based on industry guidance.
- **Zero Trust Architecture:** Enforce a policy where only verified users and devices can interact with critical application components.
- **Quality Assurance Check-ups:** Regular code reviews to identify and remediate bugs before deployment.
- **Bug Bounty Program:** Allow external researchers to identify and report vulnerabilities in AE Accelerator in exchange for monetary rewards.

---

## Scenario 3 — Phishing Attack on CFO

- **Appoint a Dedicated CISO:** Assign a leader with a strong background in cyber risk management, compliance, and security — separate from the CFO role.
- **Phishing Simulation Campaigns:** Run in-house or contracted campaigns that send fake phishing emails to employees. Those who fall for them are required to complete cybersecurity training.
- **Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) for Executives:** Require multiple verification factors for all executive accounts.
- **Distribution of Privilege:** Ensure no single individual holds complete, unchecked control over AE's IT infrastructure.
- **Incident Response Playbook:** Establish a clear escalation process for cybersecurity emergencies.
