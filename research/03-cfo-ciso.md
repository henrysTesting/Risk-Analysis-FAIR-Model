# CFO & CISO

An emergent and high-severity risk involves a targeted spear phishing attack directed at the CFO, Matthew Ellis, who — per Accel Entertainment's 10-K filing — is jointly responsible for overseeing the company's cybersecurity operations alongside the CISO. Both individuals have access to sensitive technical infrastructure information and privileged access to security system mechanisms.

Attackers could craft spear phishing emails impersonating trusted vendors, stakeholders, or internal personnel (information for which is publicly available). Given the CFO's level of access and documented lack of a formal IT/security background, he represents a high-value, high-probability target.

---

## Threat Actors

Cybercriminal syndicates, hacktivists with social engineering capabilities

---

## Potential Losses

| Threat Method | Threat Actors | Loss Type | Scale |
|---------------|---------------|-----------|-------|
| Spear phishing | Cybercriminals, hacktivists | Credential theft, unauthorized access, regulatory violations, data breach | High-Very High — direct access to financial systems, sensitive data, and security controls |
| Social engineering | Cybercriminals, hacktivists | Long-term system compromise, malware deployment, reputational damage | High — enables persistent access and manipulation of critical operations |
