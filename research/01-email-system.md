# Email System (Port 25 Open)

The email system for Accel Entertainment is at risk — Port 25 was identified as open, commonly associated with Simple Mail Transfer Protocol (SMTP) and cloud-hosted web services (Microsoft Azure in this case). This configuration leaves the system vulnerable to:

- Email spoofing
- Phishing / spam campaigns
- Malware delivery

---

## Threat Actors

Cybercriminals, script kiddies, botnets, hacktivists

---

## Potential Losses

| Threat Method | Threat Actors | Loss Type | Scale |
|---------------|---------------|-----------|-------|
| Email spoofing / phishing | Cybercriminals, Nation-state actors, Script Kiddies | Consumer information, login credentials, confidential data | Medium-High |
| Spam / DDoS attacks | Botnets, Hacktivists | Disruption of daily operations | Medium |
| Malware delivery | Advanced Persistent Threats (APTs) | Regulatory fines, reputational/brand damage | High — gaming regulations are strict; PII loss could result in $5M+ fines |
